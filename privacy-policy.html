Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VideoCrop Pro collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our desktop application and its integration with TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we may collect:

  • TikTok username and basic profile information (via user.info.basic)
  • OAuth access token and refresh token (stored locally on your device only)
  • Video files you choose to upload — processed locally before publishing

We do not collect personal data such as email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you with TikTok using the official OAuth 2.0 flow
  • To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account on your request
  • To display your TikTok profile name within the app

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

3. TikTok API Usage

This application uses the TikTok for Developers API, including Login Kit and Content Posting API. Scopes requested:

  • user.info.basic — Read basic profile info (display name, avatar)
  • video.upload — Upload video files to TikTok
  • video.publish — Publish uploaded videos to your TikTok account

4. Data Storage

All tokens and settings are stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to our servers. You may revoke access at any time via TikTok Account Settings > Connected Apps.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

If you have questions, contact us at: support@videocrop.pro
